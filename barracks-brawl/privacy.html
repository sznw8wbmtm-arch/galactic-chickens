Barracks Brawl: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Barracks Brawl ("the app") is a strategy battle game by Seif Sidhom.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store or share any personal information. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no in-app purchases. It does not need an internet connection.

Data stored on your device

Your chosen difficulty, whether you have seen the tutorial, and your sound settings are saved only on your device. We never receive this data.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, for example if online play is added in the future, we will update this page and the app's App Store privacy information.
