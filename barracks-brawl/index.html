Barracks Brawl: Support

Four castles. Three races. One banner left standing. Lead your hero, upgrade your army and destroy the enemy castles.

How to play

  • Tap the ground to move your hero. Tap an enemy to attack it.
  • Tap the round button to use your hero's power.
  • Open Upgrades to recruit more soldiers, sharpen weapons, add armour, train archers or strengthen your hero.
  • Your soldiers march out in pairs down three roads every wave. Destroy every enemy castle to win.
  • Tap II to pause. Choose Easy, Normal or Hard on the home screen.
