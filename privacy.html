Galactic Chickens: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Galactic Chickens ("the app") is an arcade game by Seif Sidhom.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store or share any personal information. The app has no accounts, no analytics and no advertising. It does not need an internet connection.

Data stored on your device

Your high score, coins, selected ship and sound settings are saved only on your device. We never receive this data.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, for example if ads are added in the future, we will update this page and the app's App Store privacy information.
